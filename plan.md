## Context 

I want to launch a P2P escrow platform on the Robinhood blockchain, called Peerly. The service will aims for offerers to
sell their tokens using an escrow contract. The contract acts as a middleman, sign of trust since a contract is immutable.


## Problem

There's so many tokens launched on-chain today. Lot of experiments. Most of them have 
- Huge trading fees
- Small liquidity causing huge impact
- LPs eater up to 5% fees

## Solution

The idea is to use a P2P escrow contract, acting as a middleman, to buy and sell your tokens at the price you desired.

## Benefits for users

- Buying/Selling tokens without dumping a chart
- Buy & Sell without having huge token fees (some tokens have up to 5% of fees)
- Buy & Sell without having huge liquidity pool fees (some LPs have up to 5% of fees)
